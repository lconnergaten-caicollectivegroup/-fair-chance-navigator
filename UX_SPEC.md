# UX Specification

## 1. Experience principles

1. **Agency first:** users choose what to share and can correct or remove it.
2. **One clear next step:** every screen has one primary action.
3. **Plain language:** avoid institutional, carceral, legalistic, or deficit-based language.
4. **Explain every match:** show why the match exists and what remains unverified.
5. **Two-sided readiness:** prepare employers as rigorously as talent.
6. **Human accountability:** clearly show where a person reviews AI output.
7. **Progress without shame:** use encouraging progress cues without punitive scoring.

## 2. Visual direction

- Vibrant CAI clean-tech aesthetic
- Deep navy foundation with electric cobalt, teal, coral, and gold accents
- White or very light-blue surfaces
- Rounded cards, soft shadows, subtle glass effects, orbit lines, and purposeful motion
- Strong contrast, large tap targets, keyboard navigation, and visible focus states
- Mobile-first Talent Orbit™; responsive desktop-first Employer Orbit™
- No prison bars, handcuffs, mugshots, court imagery, or stereotypical justice imagery

## 4. Required screens

### Screen 1 — Landing

**Headline:** From barriers to a clear next step.  
**Subhead:** Fair Chance Navigator™ prepares talent and employers, creates explainable matches, and supports the path from mutual interest to hire and retention.  
**Primary actions:** “I’m seeking opportunity” and “I’m ready to hire.”

### Screen 2 — Talent input

**Heading:** What are your goals?  
**Actions:** Upload résumé, record video profile, or enter goals manually.  
**Trust copy:** You control what is shared. Review and correct everything before employers see it.

### Screen 3 — Talent extraction review

Show extracted skills, credentials, project examples, preferences, and transcript clips as editable chips/cards. Every item has source, edit, hide, and delete controls.

### Screen 4 — Talent readiness

Assess career goals, transferable skills, communication, workplace readiness, confidence, and self-awareness. Present results as strengths and development priorities—not a pass/fail score.

### Screen 5 — Talent plan

Sections: Today, Next 7 Days, 30-Day Plan. Include résumé/profile development, storytelling, interview simulation, coaching, resource referrals, and Speed Connect™ preparation.

### Screen 6 — Opportunity match

Show employer, role, match reasons, verified requirements, development gaps, location/schedule, employer readiness badge, and “Interested / Not now.” Do not expose participant identity until mutual interest and policy conditions are met.

### Screen 7 — Employer input

**Heading:** Who are you ready to hire?  
**Input:** Enter website or paste/upload job description.  
Show extracted role purpose, essential skills, preferred skills, schedule, location, pay range, and accessibility/support commitments for employer review.

### Screen 8 — Employer readiness

Assess fair-chance practices, manager confidence, structured interviewing, onboarding, retention readiness, and leadership support. Generate an action plan and route coaching.

### Screen 9 — Talent match review

Show anonymized, skills-based profile cards. Include verified skills, readiness milestones, participant-stated interests, match explanation, and consent status.

### Screen 10 — Video profile

Show a participant-approved video player, transcript, job-relevant skill tags, and bookmarked moments. Include persistent labels: “Candidate controlled,” “Human review required,” and “No emotion or protected-trait scoring.”

### Screen 11 — Reimbursement

Card headline: “Up to 80% reimbursement.”  
Supporting copy: “For eligible staff hours through participating justice-impacted workforce programs.”  
Show source program, eligibility status, last verified date, required documents, responsible contact, and “Request eligibility review.”

### Screen 12 — Mutual match

Visualize Talent Ready + Employer Ready → Mutual Interest → Human Review → Speed Connect™ → Interview → Hire → Retain.

## 5. Motion and dynamic behavior

- Animate the orbit path only after consent and onboarding completion.
- Transform uploaded input into editable structured cards.
- Reveal match reasons progressively; do not use opaque percentage scores as the primary explanation.
- Use celebratory motion for milestones without implying guaranteed employment.
- Show live status for human review, referral, mutual interest, and reimbursement verification.

## 6. Accessibility

- WCAG 2.2 AA target
- Do not rely on color alone
- Captions and transcript for video
- Screen-reader names for all controls
- Reduced-motion preference
- Plain-language definitions for workforce and reimbursement terminology
- Save-and-return onboarding
