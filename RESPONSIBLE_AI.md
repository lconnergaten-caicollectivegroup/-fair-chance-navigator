# Responsible AI and Governance Requirements

## Human accountability

AI may organize information, generate plans, and suggest matches. Humans remain responsible for hiring, rejection, eligibility, funding, legal, medical, benefits, supervision, and crisis decisions.

## Consent

- Separate consent for résumé processing, video processing, employer visibility, referrals, evaluation, and ongoing communications.
- No bundled or prechecked consent.
- Explain purpose, data use, recipients, retention, and revocation.

## Data minimization

- Collect only information necessary for the selected service.
- Do not require a video profile.
- Provide manual and résumé-only alternatives.
- Do not expose justice history beyond participant-authorized and legally appropriate use.

## Employment-AI restrictions

- No automated selection or rejection.
- No facial analysis for emotion, personality, character, trust, or fit.
- No protected-trait inference.
- No recidivism or criminal-risk prediction.
- No hidden employer scoring.
- No match reason may be based on a proxy for protected status.

## Transparency

Every AI-generated item should display:

- What it is
- Why it was generated
- Source information
- Verification status
- How to correct it
- Whether a human reviewed it

## Quality and fairness

- Test extraction accuracy across accents, speaking styles, lighting, devices, disabilities, and language-support needs.
- Compare match and conversion rates for disparities without exposing protected data to employers.
- Maintain an incident, correction, and appeal process.
- Revalidate prompts, rules, and program data after changes.

## Security

- Role-based access
- Row-level security
- Encryption in transit and at rest
- Short-lived access links
- Access logs
- Data-retention schedule
- User-initiated deletion workflow
- Vendor and subprocessor register

## Required interface labels

- “AI-generated—review before use.”
- “Potential eligibility—verification required.”
- “Candidate controlled.”
- “Human review required.”
- “No emotion or protected-trait scoring.”
