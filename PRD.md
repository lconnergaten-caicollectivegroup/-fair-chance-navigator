# Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product

**Name:** Fair Chance Navigator™  
**Parent platform:** Fair Chance Orbit™  
**Owner:** CAI Collective Group  
**MVP type:** Human-centered, two-sided workforce-readiness and matching platform  
**Primary users:** Justice-impacted talent and fair-chance employers  
**Supporting users:** Workforce coaches, reentry navigators, program administrators, and authorized evaluators

## 2. Problem

Justice-impacted job seekers must navigate fragmented workforce, education, reentry, transportation, housing, and employer systems. Traditional job boards emphasize work-history filters and keyword matching while failing to translate transferable skills, lived experience, readiness, and support needs into a useful opportunity pathway.

Employers may want to hire fair-chance talent but lack consistent readiness standards, manager preparation, structured interviewing, onboarding support, trusted referrals, and visibility into eligible workforce reimbursements.

## 3. Product promise

Fair Chance Navigator™ learns what each side needs, helps each side become ready, creates explainable matches, and moves mutually interested users into a human-reviewed connection.

## 4. Goals

- Reduce friction between qualified justice-impacted talent and prepared employers.
- Replace unstructured screening with job-relevant, skills-based evidence.
- Shorten the time between participant readiness and employer introduction.
- Improve employer confidence and retention readiness.
- Surface partner-verified funding or reimbursement pathways without promising eligibility.
- Preserve participant consent, agency, privacy, and correction rights.
- Produce auditable recommendations that humans can review.

## 5. Non-goals

- Automated hiring, rejection, eligibility, legal, medical, benefits, supervision, or funding decisions.
- Emotion, personality, demographic, disability, criminal-risk, or protected-trait inference.
- Background-check replacement.
- Ranking people by worthiness or predicted success.
- Guaranteeing reimbursement, placement, hire, wages, or retention.

## 6. Primary journeys

### Talent Orbit™

1. Create an account and review consent choices.
2. Upload a résumé, enter goals, or record a candidate-controlled video profile.
3. Review and correct extracted skills, experience, preferences, and support needs.
4. Complete a readiness assessment.
5. Receive an individualized development plan.
6. Work with an AI Career Coach and, when available, an ICF/human coach.
7. Complete résumé/profile development, storytelling, interview simulations, and Speed Connect™ preparation.
8. Earn Fair Chance Career Ready™ status when verified criteria are met.
9. Receive explainable opportunity matches.
10. Express or decline interest privately.
11. Enter Speed Connect™ only after mutual interest and human review.
12. Track interview, hire, onboarding, and retention milestones.

### Employer Orbit™

1. Create an organization account and identify authorized users.
2. Enter a website or job description.
3. Review and correct extracted role requirements and essential skills.
4. Complete a Fair Chance employer-readiness assessment.
5. Receive a development plan covering hiring practices, manager confidence, structured interviews, onboarding, and retention.
6. Work with an AI Employer Coach and authorized human coach.
7. Earn Fair Chance Employer Ready™ status when verified criteria are met.
8. Review explainable talent matches based on verified, job-relevant criteria.
9. Review candidate-controlled video profiles only with consent.
10. Express interest without exposing the participant prematurely.
11. Review potential workforce reimbursement pathways.
12. Enter Speed Connect™, interview, hire, onboard, and track retention.

## 7. Matching requirements

Matching must be explainable and based on:

- Required and preferred job skills
- Transferable skills
- Credential and training alignment
- Schedule and location preferences
- Participant-stated interests
- Work setting preferences
- Verified readiness milestones
- Employer readiness and support capacity
- Participant-controlled support considerations

Matching must not use:

- Race, color, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, religion, national origin, disability, age, pregnancy, genetic information, or other protected characteristics
- Facial expression, emotion, personality, attractiveness, accent, or appearance scores
- Inferred criminal risk or recidivism predictions
- Unverified criminal-history assumptions

## 8. EyePop AI video-profile integration

### Permitted MVP use

- Accept a video uploaded or recorded by the participant after explicit consent.
- Create an authorized, configurable processing job.
- Extract user-approved, job-relevant structured information such as spoken skills, named tools, credentials, project examples, and transcript segments.
- Return timestamps and structured metadata that help a human navigate the video.
- Allow the participant to review, correct, hide, replace, or delete results before employer visibility.

### Prohibited use

- Emotion, personality, honesty, confidence, criminality, disability, age, race, ethnicity, gender, health, or socioeconomic inference
- Facial recognition or biometric identity matching
- Automated candidate scoring or rejection
- Silent processing without consent

## 9. Workforce reimbursement feature

The employer dashboard may display partner-supplied programs offering **up to 80% reimbursement for eligible staff hours** connected to eligible justice-impacted workforce placements.

Requirements:

- Display the responsible program/provider.
- Display jurisdiction, eligibility window, covered hours/costs, documentation, reimbursement schedule, and expiration date.
- Show “Potential eligibility—verification required.”
- Route confirmation to an authorized workforce professional.
- Record the source and last verification date.
- Never calculate or promise an award without verified program rules.

## 10. MVP success measures

- Profile completion rate
- Consent completion and correction rate
- Readiness-assessment completion
- Development-plan activation
- Match acceptance rate by both sides
- Time from readiness to first mutual match
- Speed Connect™ participation
- Interview conversion
- Hire conversion
- 30/60/90-day retention
- Reimbursement referrals and verified approvals
- User trust and usability scores
- Match disparity and false-match monitoring

## 11. MVP acceptance criteria

- A talent user can complete onboarding, upload/record a profile, correct extracted information, receive a plan, and view explainable matches.
- An employer can enter a website/job description, correct extracted requirements, complete readiness, review matches, and see verified funding guidance.
- Mutual interest is required before a connection.
- Human review is required before consequential action.
- Video analysis cannot expose prohibited inferences.
- Users can revoke consent and request deletion.
- Every AI output shows its source/status and can be corrected.
