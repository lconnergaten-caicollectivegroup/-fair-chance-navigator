## Project title

Fair Chance Navigator™

## Tagline

From barriers to a clear next step.

## Short description

Fair Chance Navigator™ is a human-centered AI concept that prepares justice-impacted talent and fair-chance employers, creates explainable skills-based matches, and supports the path from mutual interest to Speed Connect™, hire, and retention.

## Problem

People returning from incarceration often navigate disconnected employment, education, housing, transportation, and supportive-service systems. Information is difficult to interpret, and individuals may have to repeatedly disclose sensitive experiences. Employers may want to hire fair-chance talent but lack consistent readiness standards, structured interviewing, onboarding support, trusted referrals, and visibility into eligible workforce reimbursements.

## Proposed solution

Fair Chance Navigator™ provides two coordinated AI-guided experiences:

- Talent Orbit™ helps participants turn a résumé, goals, or candidate-controlled video profile into an editable readiness profile, development plan, verified resources, interview preparation, and explainable opportunity matches.
- Employer Orbit™ turns a website or job description into editable role requirements, employer-readiness actions, explainable talent matches, video-profile review, and partner-verified reimbursement guidance.

The platform connects both sides only after mutual interest and human review.

## GojiBerry-inspired interaction model

Each user begins with one simple input. AI structures that input, learns the user’s stated needs, identifies high-fit matches, and prepares the next action.

- Talent: upload résumé or record video → build readiness profile → find best-fit employers → prepare for Speed Connect™.
- Employer: enter website or job description → structure hiring needs → find Fair Chance Ready™ talent → prepare a warm introduction.

## EyePop AI capability

The proposed EyePop AI integration transforms participant-authorized video into structured, transcript-linked, job-relevant information such as explicitly stated skills, tools, credentials, work examples, and interests. Participants review and approve information before employer access. The design prohibits emotion, personality, demographic, disability, criminal-risk, or protected-trait scoring.

## Workforce reimbursement

Employer Orbit™ can surface partner-supplied programs offering up to 80% reimbursement for eligible staff hours connected to justice-impacted workforce placements. The platform shows potential eligibility, source, documentation, jurisdiction, and verification date, then routes confirmation to an authorized workforce professional. It does not guarantee funding.

## AI workflow

`INPUT → LEARN → ASSESS → DEVELOP → MATCH → REVIEW → CONNECT → HIRE → RETAIN`

## Core prompt

You are Fair Chance Navigator™, a trauma-informed workforce and reentry support assistant. Help users understand their options and prepare practical next steps using respectful, plain language. Ask for consent before collecting sensitive information and collect only what is necessary. Use only verified resources. Explain every recommendation and identify what requires human confirmation. Do not make hiring, eligibility, funding, legal, medical, benefits, housing, or supervision decisions. Do not infer criminal history, disability, emotion, personality, protected characteristics, or likelihood of success. Produce the user’s stated goal, immediate barriers, strengths, recommended actions, verified resources, documents needed, human-review questions, and a 7-day and 30-day plan. End by asking what the user wants to change, remove, or prioritize.

## Responsible AI safeguards

- Explicit and revocable consent
- Data minimization
- Participant correction and deletion rights
- Job-relevant information only
- Explainable matching
- Verified and date-stamped resources
- Human review for consequential actions
- No automated hiring or eligibility decisions
- No emotion, personality, demographic, disability, or criminal-risk inference
- Role-based access and audit logs
- Bias and disparate-impact monitoring

## Expected impact

- Reduce time required to identify appropriate services and opportunities
- Reduce repeated disclosure of sensitive information
- Improve referral and match quality
- Increase participant agency and readiness
- Improve employer readiness and speed to hire
- Connect eligible employers with verified reimbursement pathways
- Support onboarding and retention after placement

## Differentiation

Most job boards match keywords. Fair Chance Navigator™ prepares both sides, transforms user-controlled inputs into editable readiness profiles, explains why a match exists, and requires mutual interest and human review before connection.

Evaluate usability, consent comprehension, extraction accuracy, match quality, speed to connection, interview/hire conversion, retention, reimbursement approvals, user trust, and disparities.
